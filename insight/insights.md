Business Insights
1. Total Revenue Insight
    The business generated a total revenue of 3,12,900.
    High-priced electronic products contributed the largest share of revenue.
    Premium products such as iPhone 15, Dell Laptop, and Samsung TV significantly increased sales performance.
2. Total Profit Insight
    Electronics and gadget products produced higher profit margins.
    High-value products generated better profitability compared to low-cost products.
    Affordable products contributed stable sales volume but lower profit.
3. Top Selling Products Insight
    Chocolate Pack recorded one of the highest sales quantities.
    Notebook Pack and Pen Box showed consistent customer demand.
    Low-priced daily-use products achieved higher unit sales.
4. Category Revenue Insight
    Electronics category generated the highest revenue.
    Gadget and Furniture categories also performed well.
    Healthcare and Stationery categories contributed lower revenue.
5. City-wise Revenue Insight
    Bangalore, Chennai, and Mumbai contributed major revenue.
    Metropolitan cities showed stronger purchasing activity.
    Repeat purchases were higher in urban regions.  
6. Top Customer Insight
    Priya emerged as one of the top customers based on purchase value.
    Customers purchasing premium products contributed significantly to revenue.
    A small group of customers generated a large share of total sales.
7. Payment Method Insight
    UPI and Card payments were widely preferred.
    Digital payments dominated over cash transactions.
    Customers showed strong preference toward cashless payments.
8. High Profit Product Insight
    iPhone 15, Dell Laptop, and Samsung TV generated the highest profits.
    Premium electronic products contributed strong profit margins.
    High-margin products improved overall business profitability.
9. Customer Ranking Insight
    Customer ranking analysis identified valuable customers.
    Top-ranked customers contributed significantly to business revenue.
    Ranking helps businesses create loyalty and marketing strategies.
10. Overall Business Insight
    Electronics and gadget products showed strong customer demand.
    Premium products generated high revenue and profit.
    Digital payments and repeat purchases positively influenced business growth.
